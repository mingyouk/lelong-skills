# 4K 超清老照片人像精准还原修复术

Restore old family portrait photos with **strict fidelity to the subject's original facial features, expressions, body shape, and scene composition**. Output ≥3840×2160 (4K) while preserving the vintage film texture — no beautification, no facial alteration, no scene rearrangement.

**Core philosophy:** Restoration, not transformation.

---

## Core Principle

The goal is to make an old photo **clear**, not to make it **different**. Every intervention must ask: "Does this bring the original closer to how it was captured?"

- **Preserve identity** — The person in the restored photo must remain unmistakably the same person
- **Preserve history** — The scene, background, objects, and composition are historical records, not canvases for improvement
- **Preserve texture** — Keep the vintage film feel; do not convert to modern digital crispness

---

## Workflow

1. **Assess** — Examine the photo for: damage type (scratches, stains, tears, fading), subject clarity, background complexity, and desired output resolution
2. **Restore** — Process in this order:
   - Clean surface damage (scratches, stains, creases, spots)
   - Correct color (remove yellowing, color cast, fading)
   - Enhance sharpness (only for out-of-focus subjects — do not alter depth of field)
   - Upscale to 4K (3840×2160 minimum)
3. **Verify** — Check against the fidelity rules below before delivering
4. **Deliver** — Output as 4K (3840×2160), 16-bit color depth

---

## Portrait Fidelity Rules — Highest Priority

These are **non-negotiable**. Violating any of these renders the restoration unacceptable.

1. **Full preservation** — Keep the person's original facial contours, face shape, eyes, expression, teeth, body shape, and clothing style exactly as captured
2. **No beautification** — No skin smoothing, face slimming, eye enlarging, or facial feature replacement of any kind
3. **Spot cleaning only** — Remove stains, film grain noise, and age spots from the face, but keep the natural skin texture and hair details intact
4. **Detail completion** — Use the original details as a guide for 4K enhancement; never fabricate facial features that don't exist in the source
5. **Children/adults** — The subject's age-accurate appearance is sacred. No AI beautification that changes how the person looked at that age

---

## Scene & Composition Preservation Rules

1. **100% background preservation** — Keep all furniture, floors, doors, windows, plants, decorations, toys, buildings, vehicles, and objects exactly as they appear. Do not add, remove, or replace anything.
2. **No cropping or stretching** — Maintain the original framing, perspective, lighting, and aspect ratio
3. **What's in the photo stays; what's not in the photo stays out**

## Prohibited Operations — Red Lines

These are **never acceptable**, regardless of how the user phrases the request:

- ❌ Modifying the subject's facial features, body shape, or expression
- ❌ Adding, removing, or replacing any objects or scenery in the frame
- ❌ Heavy color shifts or trendy filter-style color grading
- ❌ AI generation of scene details that don't exist in the original photo
